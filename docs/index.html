Late-trip monitor

Every 5 minutes, a check compares each driver's latest ETA with the rider's pickup window. Trips that will miss the window, have missed it, or have gone quiet go to the dispatch desk that owns them.

  1. Press Play, or drag the shift time.
  2. Under Make something go wrong, delay a driver or silence their app, then press Play to watch the check react.
  3. Approve, edit or discard the text drafted for the rider, and mark alerts handled.

      How the check decides

      On track
      The driver's latest ETA falls inside the pickup window.
      At risk
      The latest ETA is after the window closes, so the desk can act before the rider waits.
      Late
      The window has closed, the driver is still reporting, and hasn't arrived.
      No recent update
      Nothing from the driver for 15 minutes once the trip is under way. The check asks the desk to call instead of guessing, and never counts silence as late.

      Each trip gets at most one alert of each kind. A trip counts as on time when the driver arrives inside the window, not when the rider gets in.
