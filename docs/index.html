Late-trip monitor

Every 5 minutes, a check compares each driver's latest ETA with the rider's pickup window. Trips that will miss the window, have missed it, or have gone quiet go to the dispatch desk that owns them. Move the shift time to replay the day.

    How the check decides

    On track
    The driver's latest ETA falls inside the pickup window.
    At risk
    The latest ETA is after the window closes, so the desk can act before the rider waits.
    Late
    The window has closed, the driver is still reporting, and hasn't arrived.
    No recent update
    Nothing from the driver for 15 minutes once the trip is under way. The check asks the desk to call instead of guessing, and never counts silence as late.

    A trip counts as on time when the driver arrives inside the window, not when the rider gets in.
